# Military and defence brand keywords - one keyword per line.
# Multi-word brands are matched by the token-all rule.
#
# Note the deliberate pairing of domain-form and bare-token keywords:
# "army.mil" cannot match "army-mil.com" because the keyword contains a
# literal dot that the typosquat replaced with a hyphen, so the bare token
# "army" is what catches that variant. Real brand lists keep both forms.

# Government and military domains
defense.gov
army.mil
navy.mil

marines.mil
spaceforce.mil
uscg.mil
nato

# Bare brand tokens (catch hyphen, dot and word-injection typosquats)

# Spanish military and defence brands. Same pairing rule as above: the keyword
# "defensa.gob.es" holds literal dots that a typosquat replaces with hyphens,
# so it cannot catch "defensa-gob-es.com" - the bare token "defensa" does that,
# and the domain-form keyword is what earns the genuine domain an 'exact' verdict.

# Official Spanish government and armed services domains
