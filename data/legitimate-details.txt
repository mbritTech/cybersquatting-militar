# Legitimate defence and military domains the filter protects.
# One domain per line. These are compared against the feed in data/domains.txt
# by edit distance, so the list holds full domains, not bare tokens: the brand
# lives in the registrable label ("army.mil" -> "army") and the TLD is ignored
# during matching, because comparing against "mil" or "com" would flag the
# TLD of nearly every domain in the feed.
#
# Only the registrable label is fuzzy-matched, so a domain with a label
# shorter than --min-length contributes nothing to detection. That affects
# "af.mil" (label "af"), which is too short to match on and is carried here
# for completeness only.

# US government and armed services
army.mil
navy.mil
af.mil
marines.mil
spaceforce.mil
uscg.mil
dod.mil
defense.gov

# Allied and international
